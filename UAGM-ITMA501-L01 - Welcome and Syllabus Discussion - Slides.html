Part 02 · What This Course Is

Data, decisions, and the gap between them.

Business Analytics covers the fundamental concepts of business data analytics: methods, models, tools, and the managerial skills to put them to work in real organizations.

PILLAR 01

Methods

Descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics. Big Data fundamentals.

PILLAR 02

Tools

Statistical software, visualization, SQL, AI and machine-learning frameworks.

PILLAR 03

Outcomes

Apply data-driven analytics to strategic decisions in local and global markets.

Part 04 · What We'll Use

Two toolchains. One workflow.

Statistics first. Machine learning second. Lab work happens in both.

Statistical Lab

For Units 1 to 4

  • SPSS or MegaStat
  • PSPP (free SPSS alternative)
  • R for advanced cases
  • Excel and Power BI for visualization
AI & ML Notebooks

For Units 5 to 8

  • Jupyter or Colab
  • scikit-learn for classical ML
  • TensorFlow or PyTorch for deep learning
  • Rapid Miner for visual workflows
Bring your laptop. By L02 you should have the statistical lab software installed and verified. Setup instructions land on the course page this week.
