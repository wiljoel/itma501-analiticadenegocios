Unit 1.1

Methods & model families

How to match a business question to the right analytical approach.

Descriptive

What happened?

Dashboards, aggregates, crosstabs. Example: monthly revenue by store.

Diagnostic

Why did it happen?

Drill-downs, cohort analysis, root-cause. Example: change in repeat purchases.

Predictive

What will happen?

Forecasting, supervised learning. Example: churn probability next quarter.

Prescriptive

What should we do?

Optimization, decision rules. Example: who to target with discounts to maximize retention.

Teaching move: walk a single business example through all four families to show dependencies and trade-offs.
Unit 1.2

The 4 V's of Big Data

Each axis changes tooling and expectations — discuss quick examples for each.

Volume

How much?

Gigabytes → petabytes. Impacts storage and compute choices.

Velocity

How fast?

Batch vs streaming, near-real-time constraints.

Variety

How many shapes?

Structured, nested JSON, images; integration work increases with variety.

Veracity

How trustworthy?

Bias, missingness, sensor drift — always evaluate before modeling.

Unit 1.3

Structured, Semi-structured, Unstructured

Definitions, examples, and preferred quick tools for each shape.

Structured

Tables

SQL tables, CSV, Excel. Tools: SQL, Jamovi, Excel, Power BI.

Semi-structured

JSON / logs

Optional fields, nested. Tools: Python, Postgres JSONB, extraction to tables.

Unstructured

Text / media

Free text, images, audio. Tools: NLP, vision models, LLMs; often used for enrichment.

Practical note: Jamovi excels with structured (table) data — pick datasets that are tidy CSVs for the lab.
